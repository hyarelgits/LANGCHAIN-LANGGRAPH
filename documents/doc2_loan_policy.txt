LOAN POLICY - EXCEPTION RULE

Exception rule:
If CIBIL score is only about 30 points less
than the required score (for example 680 to 690),
AND the user has 1 year bank statement,
then the bank can offer the loan with conditions.

If the user does NOT have 1 year bank statement,
then the result is Not Eligible.

Final answer must explain:
1. First condition result
2. Exception rule
3. Bank statement check
4. Final decision
